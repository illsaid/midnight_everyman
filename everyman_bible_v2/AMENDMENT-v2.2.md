# Midnight Everyman House Style Bible v2.2 Amendment

**Status:** Canonical amendment  
**Applies to:** House Style Bible revision 02 and amendment v2.1  
**Scope:** Character-production priority and generated-motion boundary  

This amendment preserves Bible v2 and v2.1 except where the rules below narrow the role of generated performance plates.

## Revised production principle

**Everyman is a reusable replacement-cel system.** Static held poses and short pose sequences are the dependable production grammar. Remotion stages, times, mirrors, scales and composites complete drawings; it does not deform character anatomy.

Generated character video is an optional exception. It is never a required layer, quota, prerequisite or critical path for an episode.

## Production order

1. Select an approved held pose from the semantic library.
2. If the beat requires a gesture, build a short sequence from approved held poses or commission the minimum missing key pose.
3. Redesign the beat if static staging, props, camera, typography and sound can communicate it more economically.
4. Consider generated video only for an unusually valuable action that cannot be communicated acceptably by the first three steps.

## Pose-library contract

- Maintain approximately 20-30 broadly reusable, clearly named held poses before expanding motion systems.
- Store each cel as a transparent PNG with measured anchor, scale, facing, source and approval metadata.
- Generate related poses as master sheets to improve identity consistency.
- Human approval is required before a candidate becomes canonical.
- Treat run, jump and similar dynamic drawings as expressive key poses, not claims of continuous animation.
- Mirror the complete cel in Remotion when the opposite facing is required.

## Generated-motion boundary

Generated video may be used occasionally when it adds enough value to justify its generation, selection, cleanup and integration cost. A scene must not be designed around an unverified clip, and the surrounding set must not require reconstruction to accommodate generator drift.

If the clip fails, the episode must still be producible with the cel system. The existing performance-plate scaffold remains an archived experimental lane, not the next production dependency.

## Superseded v2.1 language

- The three-level hierarchy is narrowed to a two-level production default: held cel and limited cel sequence.
- Performance plates are no longer a routine third tier or a planned episode allocation.
- The provisional target of three-to-six performance plates per episode is withdrawn.
- The immediate performance-plate validation is no longer the next project milestone.

## Unchanged canon

- The skeletal SVG puppet remains rejected.
- Character anatomy is never created by rotating disconnected limbs.
- Complete replacement drawings provide expression and body mechanics.
- Remotion remains the deterministic staging, information-design, compositing and rendering engine.
- Props, backgrounds, camera, typography and sound may add motion around held character drawings.
- One dominant visual action per beat remains mandatory.
- The human quality gate remains final.

## Immediate validation

Approve or revise the 24-pose Observer catalog, then build two reusable action packs from approved keys: `run-away` and `jump-for-joy`. Validate them inside one short narrated scene without generated character video.
