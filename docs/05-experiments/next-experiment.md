## Objective

Build one complete 5–8 minute Hidden Systems episode using the existing
Midnight Everyman systems-explainer architecture. Record owner time by phase and
identify the reusable component delta.

This replaces the proposed second 45–75 second proof. The 63-second wrong-fuel
composition already validates the screen language; the unresolved question is
whether the architecture survives a complete publishable episode.

## Pre-build gates

- At least 12 candidates pass touched and opaque.
- Three candidates have a selected rung-three title, thumbnail moment and
  opening visual.
- Each package makes the human vulnerability or violated assumption legible and
  can stand alone without channel history.
- The selected episode's technical and incident claims have literal sources.
- The user approves the final package and script before full animation.

## Preferred candidate

Lift governor and safety gear, subject to source verification and packaging.
Aircraft oxygen-mask generator and airbag sensor are the comparison packages.

## Format

- 1920 × 1080
- 24 fps
- 300–480 seconds
- Six narrative beats
- Approximately 13 movements of 20–45 seconds
- Final voice-over is the timing authority

## Constraints

- Reuse the existing systems graphics, Observer cels and bounded-effect
  architecture before creating new primitives.
- Use Everyman only for reaction, emphasis, scale or an editorial button.
- Do not depend on exact character/object contact.
- Keep one dominant informational action per visual beat.
- Use purpose-built diagrams rather than stock-footage assembly.
- Preserve a clean composition path alongside any FX pass.
- Do not change the global frame rate to 30 fps.

## Measurements

- Source research and fact checking
- Title and thumbnail packaging
- Script and voice-over
- Scene manifest and storyboard
- New technical assets
- Reused assets and components
- Remotion assembly
- Correction and QA
- Machine render time, recorded separately

## Pass criteria

- The episode makes its mechanism understandable without relying on narration
  alone.
- The package and first eight seconds make the same promise.
- No safety or mechanism claim lacks provenance.
- The full composition renders successfully and passes visual QA.
- Owner time and reuse fraction indicate a credible path toward the eight-hour
  mature workflow, even if the first build exceeds it.

## Decision after test

- **Pass:** use the architecture for two more episodes and set a provisional
  cadence from measured owner time.
- **Partial:** revise only the primitives or workflow stages that caused repeat
  repair, then run a second full pilot.
- **Fail:** reduce visual density or change the episode model before building
  automation or expanding the candidate pool.
