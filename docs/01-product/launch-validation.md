# Launch validation

This governs audience learning after publication. It does not authorize a
publish action; final publication remains a human decision.

## Organic test boundary

Keep launch validation organic. Do not buy promotional views during the pilot
or initial audience-learning block. Paid promotion is withheld because it tests
paid acquisition against a selected audience rather than organic packaging and
recommendation fit. It is **not** withheld because promotion is assumed to
damage the channel algorithmically.

YouTube states that Promote does not affect organic recommendations, while
watch hours and subscribers acquired through ad campaigns generally do not
count toward YouTube Partner Program eligibility:
<https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12199105>

## Read analytics at their real strength

There is no canonical 200-views-per-day threshold. YouTube does not publish a
universal sufficient-data cutoff, and some reports suppress low-volume detail
at unpublished thresholds:
<https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9101241>

- Read click-through rate against impressions and traffic source, not views per
  day.
- Treat early retention cliffs and click-through movement as directional until
  the audience mix and sample stabilize.
- Do not act on small percentage changes without enough observations to
  distinguish them from noise.
- Prefer native concurrent thumbnail testing to sequential manual swaps.
- Separate a weak package from a narrow topic and from a weak episode; the same
  metric can result from different failures.

## Comments are qualitative evidence

Use comments to collect:

- language viewers repeat;
- moments they describe emotionally;
- explanations they did not understand;
- questions that point toward adjacent episodes.

Comments describe the minority who chose to respond. They do not represent
non-clickers, silent viewers or the recommendation system, and they do not
replace retention or traffic-source analysis.

## Scale-aware checkpoints

- **Pilot:** validates the production system. It predicts no audience result.
- **Early uploads:** diagnose packages and comprehension without declaring a
  permanent niche from one outcome.
- **Six-episode review:** examine repeated audience overlap, native thumbnail
  tests, retention patterns, comment language and owner time before changing the
  channel contract.

YouTube describes performance through appeal, engagement and satisfaction, and
states that individual underperformance does not impose a channel-wide penalty:
<https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16559650>

## Owned audience

Do not launch an email product merely to “own the audience.” Add an
off-platform relationship only when repeated viewing and comment evidence
reveals a recurring value proposition that continues between episodes. Define
that product, its owner-time cost and its revenue path before adding calls to
action to the videos.
