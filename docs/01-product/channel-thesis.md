# Channel thesis

## Goal

Create a faceless, revenue-oriented YouTube channel whose production system can reach one video per week at approximately eight hours per completed episode.

## Working content model

The strongest current direction is information compression applied to subjects with immediate human-interest tension. The format is a container, not the niche. Topic selection must begin with the psychology of a general late-night scroller rather than an enthusiast already interested in the subject.

### Current working premise - directional

The container has been narrowed to a working premise: **"what happens if you use the wrong X"**. See `premise-wrong-x.md` for the reasoning, the risks and the scored pilot slate, and `D-015` in the decision record.

This is directional and subject to adjustment. It is adopted so that topic selection, script structure and cel-library construction share a target, not because it has been validated. Nothing has been published under it.

The premise was chosen as much for production economics as for editorial strength: "every type of X" spends art in proportion to the length of its list, whereas "wrong X" is satisfied by a bounded contrast and repeats the same central character beat every episode, so library reuse compounds.

The research rationale and named examples are preserved in `youtube-subject-research.md`; the operational selection method is in `topic-selection-framework.md`; the current working premise and its pilot slate are in `premise-wrong-x.md`.

Useful topic promises include:

- A large or confusing world made comprehensible quickly
- Hidden rules governing an ordinary but consequential system
- A comparison that reveals danger, status, money, survival or absurdity
- A complete taxonomy with escalating surprises
- A familiar object whose variations have unexpected consequences

The working formulation is:

> Engaging subject matter shoehorned into a repeatable format, not arbitrary subject matter rescued by a format.

Dry completeness is insufficient. A topic needs a human stake visible in the title and thumbnail.

## Fixed constraints

- Faceless; no use of the creator's likeness
- AI voice, transformed creator voice or conventional voice-over are acceptable
- Weekly publishing target
- Eight-hour steady-state production ceiling
- Existing tools include CapCut, ElevenLabs and major general-purpose AI subscriptions
- Some expenditure on generated imagery or video is acceptable
- The visual format must be more stimulating than generic B-roll and pan-and-scan editing

## Business success

Success is not views alone. The channel must eventually balance:

- Click potential among a broad audience
- Retention and repeatability
- Advertising value and additional monetization options
- Research burden
- Asset-generation burden
- Editing burden
- Sustainable creator interest
- Ability to publish without a production team

## Unresolved product decisions

- Final niche or topic territory - narrowed but not locked; see `premise-wrong-x.md`
- Video duration - short-form vertical is being tested first; see `D-016`
- Host voice strategy
- Revenue model beyond advertising
- Channel name, packaging system and upload cadence during launch
- Whether the first pilot should test a taxonomy, hidden-system or consequence-comparison topic

Do not lock a niche until the production-format test establishes a realistic cost per finished minute.
