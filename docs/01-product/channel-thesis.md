# Channel thesis

## Goal

Create a faceless, revenue-oriented YouTube channel whose production system can
eventually reach one polished weekly episode in approximately eight owner-hours.

## Launch territory

**Hidden Systems:** familiar engineered mechanisms that protect ordinary people
when something fails.

The boundary is the failure-catching mechanism, not an industry. A lift
governor, circuit breaker, airbag sensor and aircraft oxygen-mask generator can
belong to the same channel because they satisfy the same curiosity: an ordinary
object contains a consequential mechanism the viewer cannot explain.

## Working thesis

Purpose-built visuals, disciplined subject selection and a sustainable cadence
may produce a stronger performance floor than daily explainers assembled from
stock material. Those factors co-vary across the four verified channel samples;
public competitor data cannot isolate causality.

The first full pilot tests production viability, not expected views.

## Product

- Faceless, narrator-led, horizontal long-form
- 1920 × 1080 at 24 fps
- 5–8 minutes
- One object, one mechanism, six narrative beats
- Purpose-built vector schematics and information motion
- Everyman used selectively for reaction, scale and dry editorial counterpoint
- Three thumbnail concepts per episode
- Twenty title candidates, one selected rung-three title

## Subject admission

Three hard gates determine production readiness:

1. Touched
2. Opaque
3. Packageable

Case evidence is recorded separately from action state. A documented incident
may improve priority or narrative treatment, but no measured evidence shows that
incident framing outperforms straight process explanation.

## Business success

Success is revenue per owner-hour, not views alone. Evaluate:

- Click and retention performance
- Research and fact-check burden
- Finished minutes per owner-hour
- Reuse fraction and correction time
- Advertising and non-AdSense monetization compatibility
- Ability to publish without a production team

## Unresolved decisions

- Cadence
- Synthetic versus recorded narration
- Public channel name
- Failure-led versus process-led opening
- Revenue model beyond advertising
- Whether the eventual eight-hour ceiling is achievable

Do not resolve these through competitor inference when the pilot can measure
them directly.
