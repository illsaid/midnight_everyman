One curve, everywhere cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1) Symmetric, mechanical, no overshoot. Nothing bounces — a bounce implies elasticity the subject does not have. Specified in frames, not milliseconds. Remotion is frame-based and the project runs at 24 fps, so every value here is a whole frame count. The old millisecond figures landed on 9.12, 2.16, 14.4, 5.76, 21.6, 28.8 and 9.6 frames — all of which would silently round.
Draw in, never fade 9f + 2f
Elements enter by drawing their own stroke, staggered in the order the script names them.
Camera moves, model doesn't 14f
Scale and translate on the stage group. Within a movement.
State change is colour only 6f
Geometry holds still and the stroke crossfades. Moving and recolouring at once reads as two events.
Flow marches 30f linear
Dash offset with the direction of travel. Speed carries pressure — never add a second cue.
Hold before the payoff 10f still
Complete stillness before the failure fires. The cheapest tension the format has.
Movement transition new 21f
A larger camera move between movements. Deliberately slower than the 600 — the viewer must feel a section end.
720f The thirty-second rule. No single frame state may hold longer than 720 frames without a camera move, a state change, or a new element drawing in. At forty-five seconds this was impossible to violate. At six minutes it is the default failure — and stillness is where retention dies. The 2-frame stagger also lands on the 12 fps step grid the Observer cels use, so both grids agree.

The movement is the unit, not the beat Scaling a 45-second budget by eight gives a 95-second beat, and no continuous camera move survives 95 seconds. So beats subdivide into movements of 480–1080 frames (20–45 s), each with its own arrival, dwell and exit. Thirteen per episode. The script is written to movements — sheet 11 — and the Remotion timeline is one Sequence per movement, which is what makes the build reusable. Note "beat" is overloaded: six narrative beats here, against the 3–5 second shot beats in visual-language.md.
