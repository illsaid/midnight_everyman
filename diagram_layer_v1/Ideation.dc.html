Ideation protocol

Sheet 10 / 11

Everything here is ceiling work — it raises the best case. It runs after sheet 11, never instead of it. A candidate that fails the two gates does not enter this process however good the outlier signal looks.

1
The Monday scan · 20 min Fifteen to twenty tracked channels. For each, compare the newest video against the mean of its previous five — never the channel's lifetime average, which is polluted by seasonality, by the creator's own improvement, and by idea decay. Flag anything above 1.5×. Within three days of upload is the signal window.

Climb the abstraction ladder Never copy a winning title. The incumbent beats you on resources in their own niche. Take two winners, strip to what they share, then re-specify into yours.
“7 levels of rapper” + “7 levels of fantasy book” ↓ strip to what is shared N LEVELS OF [THING I CONSUME] ↓ push one rung wider N LEVELS OF [THING EVERYONE HAS] → dreams · language · sleep · pain

Cap trend-chasing at 1 in 10 Riding a news surge works and decays in a fortnight. This catalogue is built to re-spike for years. Spend the slot only when a real event lands inside the subject area — a collapse, a grounding, a grid failure.
Chasing outliers cannot raise a floor A tracked outlier tells you what spiked once, not what a mediocre episode will earn. Channels die at the floor, not the ceiling — so spend the scan on format transfer, and let sheet 11 pick the subjects.
