Schematic House Style

Human-scale questions answered by making the hidden system visible. One continuous animated model per episode, built under three rules. This canvas is the diagram layer; House Style Bible v2 governs the Observer, palette and texture.

1
Colour is state, never decoration Structure stays neutral. Colour marks what the system is doing right now — and only that.
2
The stage transforms, it never cuts Beats move the camera and change the model's state. They never replace the picture.
3
Line weight is meaning Six weights, six fixed meanings. A viewer who has seen three episodes reads the fourth unaided.
Why they hold. YouTube converges on an audience over your first few videos — it shows video 1 to a mixed set, watches who clicks, and narrows from there. Breaking a rule doesn't cost momentum. It de-converges a model built over months. The one exception: one episode in five may vary the format deliberately — never the audience. A system that can only produce its median never produces its outlier.
