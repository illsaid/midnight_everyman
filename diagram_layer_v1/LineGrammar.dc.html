Line grammar

Sheet 03 / 11

Six weights, six fixed meanings. This is the part that compounds: after three episodes a dashed line has always meant the same thing, so the fourth needs no narration to be read.

Enclosure 6 px · ink The outer boundary of the physical thing. One per diagram, and the only line that may carry a flat cutaway fill.
Component 3 px · structural Internal parts a person could point at if the housing were open.
Hidden 3 px dash 14/9 Behind a wall, underground, inside a sealed housing. The channel's whole subject, so it earns its own weight.
Reference 2 px dot 2/7 Dimensions, centrelines, timelines. Never the subject — only the measuring of it.
Active path 5 px · semantic The only weight permitted to take a colour. Whatever is happening right now.
Flow 5 px dash 22/22, marching Active path in motion. Dash offset runs at 1.2 s linear, always with the direction of travel.
Reading together — a valve in a wall
Never introduce a seventh. If a diagram needs a distinction these six cannot carry, the diagram is doing too much — split the beat rather than extend the vocabulary.
