Rebuilt for 16:9 on 2 Sep 2026, then reconciled against the Midnight Everyman system. The vertical frame had no room for anything but the model. Horizontal buys a persistent annotation column — and at six minutes that column is what stops the viewer forgetting what a part was called.

The ledger is the format Every part name enters the ledger when it is first drawn and stays there for the rest of the video. Nine entries maximum. If a tenth is needed, the subject is too big for one episode.
No title card Long-form does not need one — the browser tab already carries the title. Open cold on the object. The vertical frame's title band is deleted, not resized.
Everyman is not in this layout He pops into frame with the diagram around or behind him — mostly at the open and the close. Those beats replace the whole composition rather than occupying a zone inside it. This geometry governs the diagram passages only; no space is reserved for him.
The last 20 seconds End screens cover the ledger and the lower right. Nothing load-bearing goes there in the final 20 s — that is where the takeaway card must not sit.
