## Shared prompt contract

Original flat midcentury commercial cel illustration; sparse dark linework,
simplified geometric forms and subtle paper grain. Neutral-dominant warm paper,
ink, reference grey, ink-mute and cutaway beige. Preserve the approved Observer
identity and costume only where he is the principal commuter. Avoid large
environmental areas of the exact teal, mustard or coral accents. No embedded
text, logos, arrows, exposed internal machinery, photorealism or dramatic lens
effects. Preserve neutral negative space for Remotion state overlays.

## Unit prompt deltas

### G-01 — Incident reversal

Wide three-quarter view from the lower landing of a crowded upward escalator in
a simplified mall. One Observer-like figure is readable near the front third;
everybody is calm and balanced at the instant before reversal. Keep the travel
direction and both landings unambiguous; reserve the upper-left for evidence.

### G-02 — Lace approach

Low three-quarter close view of the Observer's lower legs and black shoes. One
loose black lace lies on the ribbed tread approaching, but not touching, a pale
desaturated yellow comb plate. The shoe and lace occupy the lower-right; reserve
the upper-left for the failure label.

### G-03 — Step sag

Medium-wide view along a descending escalator. The Observer rides in the left
third and looks toward one still-level step immediately ahead that can later
animate downward. Reserve the upper-right for the failure label; show no sag,
hole or exposed machinery in the anchor.

### G-04 — Ordinary ride

Side three-quarter view of the lower landing. The relaxed Observer approaches
from stage left, one step before boarding. Keep the comb, skirt edge and safety
brush readable and leave an uncluttered wall for the final hidden-systems reveal.
