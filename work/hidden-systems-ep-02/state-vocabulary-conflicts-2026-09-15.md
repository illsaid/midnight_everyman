# State vocabulary vs the generated-media plan — two conflicts before the contact sheet

Raised 15 Sep 2026, after `diagram-layer.md` gained the scene-state colour
vocabulary in `0112eda`. Both items are cheap now and expensive after four plates
are generated.

## The vocabulary is good

Worth recording why, since it is now canon:

- **"A safety device that fails to fire must not animate into its tripped pose."**
  The Hong Kong beat is now protected by a rule rather than by whoever remembers
  it. This is the single best line in the table.
- **"At most one semantic colour at a time, except during an explicit six-frame
  state handoff."** Stops the frame becoming a christmas tree once four failure
  modes are on screen.
- Mustard deliberately shared between normal motion and approaching-limit, with
  the ambiguity **stated** and resolved by form and role rather than pretended
  away.

## Conflict 1 — the generated plates are told to use the state colours

`generated-media-plan.md`, Shared visual contract:

> "House paper, ink, **teal, mustard and coral** palette; no photorealism."

That file predates today's update. As of `0112eda` those three are no longer
decorative house colours — they are the exclusive state channel:

> "Teal always means a protection worked. Coral always means breakage, failed
> protection or consequence."

So a generated plate containing teal or coral asserts a state. G-01 is the
incident reversal; if that plate carries coral as atmosphere it collides with the
coral that means *this device failed* — in the exact scene where that meaning is
doing the most work. A mustard coat on a commuter in G-02 reads as motion or
approaching limit.

**Fix, before generating:** amend the shared visual contract to the **neutral base
range only** — paper, ink, reference, ink-mute, cutaway. Remotion owns every
semantic colour on top. That keeps the rule the whole film now depends on:
anything coloured means something, and one system applies it.

This costs the plates nothing. The plan already assigns them people, cloth and
atmosphere while "Remotion supplies mechanisms and causal proof" — humans in a
neutral range is the house look regardless.

## Conflict 2 — the Observer wears coral, and coral is now load-bearing

The plan says:

> "Use the approved Observer reference where a principal commuter is visible."

The approved Observer wears a coral jacket. Under the new table coral means
breakage, failed protection or consequence. Every plate containing the Observer
therefore puts the failure colour on the protagonist, permanently, in a film whose
climax is a protection that failed.

This conflict was recorded during Pilot 01 as a house-rule-1 problem and was
tolerable while colour was loose. The state vocabulary makes it load-bearing.

Three ways out, in my order of preference:

1. **Recolour the Observer into the neutral range for Episode 02 plates.** The
   cels are approved for identity, not for palette; a neutral variant keeps the
   silhouette and frees coral entirely.
2. **Carve out an explicit exception** — "character costume is outside the state
   channel" — and state it in `diagram-layer.md` so no one has to rediscover it.
   Cheapest, but it weakens the rule exactly where the rule is newest.
3. Leave it. Not recommended: the first time the Observer stands next to a coral
   failed device, the two reds mean different things in the same frame.

This one needs an owner decision. Option 1 is a cel job, not a redesign.

## Minor — Pilot 01 predates the vocabulary

The sprinkler bulb is permanently coral. Under the new table a `safety-device` at
rest is neutral. Pilot 01 is published and cannot change, and at four views the
cost of the inconsistency is zero — but a one-line note in `diagram-layer.md`
saying the vocabulary begins at Episode 02 would stop a future reader concluding
the canon was broken.

## On the contact sheet itself

The approval rule is right — all four anchors together, then one unit at a time.
One addition: the contact sheet should be checked **against the state table**, not
only against itself. Four plates that agree with each other and sit outside the
palette will pass a self-consistency review and fail in the cut, which is the
expensive place to find it.
