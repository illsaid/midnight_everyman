# HG-01 — Cinema Believer

**Anchor status: approved 4 Sep 2026.**

**Purpose:** Test whether the canonical Observer survives generated motion inside
a dark scenic environment while retaining the house style.

## Editorial use

- Cue: 01
- VO: “You’ve seen it in movies a hundred times.”
- Editorial window: 0:00.00–0:02.27
- Requested source length: 4–6 seconds
- Anchor: `../anchors/hg-01-cinema-anchor-v1.png`
- Identity reference: `../canonical/observer-pose-sheet-v2.png`

## Image-reference prompt

Use the anchor as the exact composition, character-identity and style reference.
Preserve the canonical Observer’s bald oval head, three hair strokes, long simple
nose, dot eyes, dark side hair, teal jacket, white shirt, narrow black tie, coral
trousers and black shoes. Preserve the cinema architecture, seat geometry, paper
texture, palette, lighting direction and 16:9 framing.

## Motion prompt

Locked camera. The Observer remains seated and absorbed by the unseen movie.
His eyes track the screen slightly, his delighted expression breathes naturally,
and the cool projected light flickers very gently across his face and jacket.
Seat rows and architecture remain completely stable. Restrained two-dimensional
limited animation; no large gesture and no camera move.

## Avoid

- No additional people or faces
- No wardrobe, hair, head-shape or facial redesign
- No hand mutation or extra fingers
- No moving seats, walls or projector window
- No sprinkler, lighter, water or text in this source
- No photorealism, 3D lighting or glossy surfaces
- No zoom, orbit, handheld motion or rack focus

## Delivery and edit plan

- Preserve the full 16:9 frame; do not pre-crop to the 2.27-second cue.
- Use the strongest 2–3-second span at cue 01.
- Hold the clean first or last frame if more time is required.
- Remotion may add controlled screen flicker; the generated plate need not carry
  the entire lighting effect.

## Acceptance gate

- Observer identity remains stable for the complete usable span.
- Mouth motion remains minimal and does not resemble speech.
- Seat backs and projector beam do not warp.
- At least one clean frame exists at both ends.
- The moving version is materially more engaging than a slow push on the anchor.

## Deterministic fallback

Use the anchor as a held plate with a 3% Remotion push, subtle two-frame screen
flicker and the existing paper-grain treatment.
