## Image-reference prompt

Use the anchor as the exact set, composition, character-identity and style
reference. Preserve the canonical Observer, acoustic ceiling grid, single ceiling
head, window, desk, doorway, tool case, bulb tray, warm paper texture, restrained
palette and locked 16:9 perspective. The tray and case are contextual set pieces,
not objects for detailed manipulation.

## Motion prompt

Locked wide camera. Begin with the Observer quietly studying the ceiling. He
shifts his gaze between the room and the sprinkler head, pauses, and shows a small
uncertain change of expression. After a clean held beat, he turns and walks toward
the doorway, leaving the room behind. Keep his hands away from the bulb tray,
tool case and sprinkler. Preserve clean pauses before the turn and after he exits
so the source can be divided into three edit spans.

## Avoid

- No picking up, weighing or installing a bulb
- No ladder and no hand-to-prop contact
- No sprinkler activation, smoke, flame or water
- No additional people
- No moving ceiling grid, desk, window, case or bulb tray
- No changing clothing, facial identity or body proportions
- No comedic mugging, shrugging or exaggerated pantomime
- No camera pan, zoom, orbit or handheld motion
- No text, labels, logos or signage

## Delivery and edit plan

- Span A: stable upward study for cue 69.
- Span B: uncertainty and room survey for cue 74.
- Span C: turn/exit or a held empty-room frame for cue 75.
- The kitchen and quiet-office comparisons interrupt these spans in the final
  edit, so exact continuity between A and B is unnecessary.
- Accurate bulb choice and sprinkler geometry remain Remotion overlays.

## Acceptance gate

- Same Observer identity in all three usable spans.
- Ceiling, door, window and desk remain fixed.
- No object contact or hand defects.
- A clean hold exists before the exit.
- The empty room remains usable after the character leaves.
- At least two of the three intended edit spans survive without repainting.

## Deterministic fallback

Use the static office anchor with approved `inspectForward` and `shrugConfused`
cels, then remove the cel for the empty-room departure hold.
