## Image-reference prompt

Use the anchor as the exact composition and object-style reference. Preserve the
intact glass tube, exactly one visible bubble, red-orange liquid level, brass
frame, crop, perspective, dark teal pressure field, warm paper texture and 16:9
framing. This is a poetic macro, not the explanatory mechanism shot.

## Motion prompt

Nearly locked camera with an extremely slow forward drift. A restrained highlight
travels across the intact glass and brass. The single bubble remains one bubble
and may compress almost imperceptibly without vanishing. The liquid, frame and
background remain stable. Build quiet tension; nothing breaks and no water is
released.

## Avoid

- Exactly one bubble: no splitting, duplication or extra droplets
- No shattering, cracking, leaking or spraying
- No fire, smoke, people, labels or text
- No new mechanical parts or geometry changes
- No liquid-level jump or colour change
- No camera shake, orbit, focus hunt or fast zoom
- No photorealistic chrome, glass-product rendering or 3D look
- No literal river or surreal imagery

## Delivery and edit plan

- Use the opening span for cue 91 and continue through cue 92.

## Acceptance gate

- Exactly one bubble throughout the usable span.
- Bulb and brass geometry remain stable.
- No accidental activation or material morphing.
- The slow movement remains visible but subordinate to the VO.
- The plate can accept Remotion pressure and lighting overlays cleanly.

## Deterministic fallback

Use the anchor as a still with an 8% Remotion push, controlled rim-light sweep,
subtle pressure-field drift and existing paper grain.
